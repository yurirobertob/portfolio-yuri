Birinight

Social-first design for a ready-to-drink brand aimed at a young, party-occasion audience.

My role
Art direction, social campaign design
Client
Birinight — Sol Bebidas
Deliverables
Social media artwork, campaign pieces

Birinight is a ready-to-drink mixed alcoholic beverage from Sol Bebidas — the same producer behind Vulcano. It targets a young audience and party occasions, positioned around convenience, refreshment and value. The pieces below are from the brand’s social channels, where the visual language had to stay loud enough to compete in-feed without losing the product as the hero.

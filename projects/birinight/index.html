Birinight

Social-first artwork for a ready-to-drink brand built around party occasions.

Client
Birinight, Sol Bebidas
Role
Art direction, social campaign
Deliverables
Social media artwork, campaign pieces

The brief

Birinight is a ready-to-drink mixed alcoholic beverage from Sol Bebidas, the same producer behind Vulcano. It targets a young audience and party occasions, positioned on convenience and value. These pieces ran on the brand’s social channels, where the artwork had to compete in a crowded feed without burying the product.

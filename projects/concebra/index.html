Triunfo Concebra

Institutional communication for a road infrastructure operator — a very different register from the beverage work.

My role
Art direction, institutional content design
Client
Triunfo Concebra — Triunfo Participações e Investimentos
Deliverables
Institutional artwork, campaign and social content

Triunfo Concebra operates one of Brazil’s major road concessions, managing a logistics corridor connecting the Federal District, Goiás and Minas Gerais, as a subsidiary of Triunfo Participações e Investimentos. The communication here had to carry institutional credibility and public-safety messaging — a deliberately restrained visual register compared to the consumer work above.

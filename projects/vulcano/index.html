Vulcano Energy Drink

Campaign and social design for one of Brazil’s established energy drink brands.

My role
Art direction, key visuals, social campaign design
Client
Vulcano — Sol Bebidas
Deliverables
Key visuals, campaign artwork, social assets

Vulcano is a Brazilian energy drink produced by Sol Bebidas, based in Senador Canedo, Goiás. Launched in 2008, it has grown into one of the category’s established names in Brazil, with a particularly strong presence across the Centre-West and Northeast regions. The work below covers key visuals and campaign pieces built around the brand’s heat-and-energy territory.

Triunfo Concebra

Institutional and public-safety communication for a road concession. A much quieter register than the drinks work.

Client
Triunfo Concebra, Triunfo Participações
Role
Art direction, institutional content
Deliverables
Institutional artwork, campaign and social content

The brief

Triunfo Concebra runs one of Brazil’s major road concessions, managing a corridor that connects Brasília, Goiás and Minas Gerais, as part of Triunfo Participações. The communication carried institutional credibility and public-safety messaging, so the visual language pulled back hard from the noise the consumer work needed.

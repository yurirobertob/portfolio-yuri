About

I design products and then I ship them.

Product designer in Goiânia, Brazil, working with teams across the UK, US and Europe. Five-plus years in art direction, now building software end to end.

How I got here

I spent most of my career as an art director and graphic designer, working on brand and campaign work for consumer and infrastructure clients. That's where the craft came from — type, composition, knowing when something is finished.

In 2022 I moved to London for a year, and it changed what I paid attention to. I stopped seeing visual briefs and started seeing broken workflows. The clearest one: cleaning-team managers running entire operations out of WhatsApp threads, with no tool built for teams that size. That observation eventually became Shiftsly.

What followed wasn't a portfolio of concepts. It was three products that actually went live — with authentication, payment providers, databases, scheduled jobs and, eventually, users who noticed when something broke.

What that means in practice

Most design portfolios end at the handoff. Mine doesn't, because in every project here there was nobody to hand off to. I made the interface decisions and the schema decisions, and those two things turn out to constrain each other constantly.

That has a specific consequence I'd argue is the most useful thing about working with me: I don't design things that can't be built, and I don't discover the cost of a decision after someone else has committed to it. When I propose a flow, I already know roughly what it costs to implement, where the state has to live, and what happens when the network call fails.

It also means I've been on the wrong end of my own decisions in production — which is a faster teacher than any critique.

Capabilities

Backend & data

Payments & subscriptions

Production & operations

Deployment, cron scheduling, monitoring, alerting and incident response on systems with paying users.

Growth instrumentation

Conversion tracking, attribution through checkout, analytics dashboards, and automated reporting loops.

Working with AI

Vulcano

Key visuals and campaign artwork for one of Brazil’s established energy drink brands.

Client
Vulcano, Sol Bebidas
Role
Art direction, key visuals, social campaign
Deliverables
Key visuals, campaign artwork, social assets

The brief

Vulcano is a Brazilian energy drink produced by Sol Bebidas in Senador Canedo, Goiás. It launched in 2008 and grew into one of the category’s established names, with a strong presence across the Centre-West and Northeast. The work below covers key visuals and campaign pieces built around the brand’s heat territory, where the can had to stay the hero at any crop.
